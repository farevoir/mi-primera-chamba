# Confirmed skills

Skills that are not in the base CV but that you confirmed you have. Only what is listed under **Confirmed** may be added to a tailored CV beyond the base CV.

Format: `- **Skill** — level / real context in your own words (confirmation date)`

## To learn
Skills you haven't used but could learn. They stay out of CVs until you practice them and confirm; in an interview you can say you're learning them.

## Declined
Skills already asked about that you don't have. Don't ask again.
